## Limits

Focus assertions run only after explicit focus and selection actions; the checker does not infer focus behavior from pointer clicks or disabled controls. Runs cover serialized actions in Chromium against the loopback HTTP lab. IME composition, concurrent browser scheduling, other browser engines, pixel equivalence, and production services remain untested.
