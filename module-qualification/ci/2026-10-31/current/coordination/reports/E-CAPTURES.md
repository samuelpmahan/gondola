## Scope limits

This is a finite qualification of one compiled parent/child fixture, one fixed scenario, three deterministic 40-event seeds, and the captured React/DOM/swap profiles. The checker runs no JavaScript, browser, or Node subprocess; it validates browser-produced evidence but does not independently launch a browser. It is not a claim about arbitrary modules, arbitrary event streams, other renderers, or production deployment.
